## Recovery checklist
- [ ] Establish incident scope and safety of the environment.
- [ ] Identify a known-good backup and verify integrity.
- [ ] Restore network, identity, DNS, and storage dependencies as needed.
- [ ] Restore workloads in documented priority order.
- [ ] Validate data consistency, access control, and monitoring.
- [ ] Document timeline, gaps, and follow-up actions.
